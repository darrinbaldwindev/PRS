## Purpose

Provide a durable, evidence-first register for PRS buyer validation. This file is a capture structure, not market evidence. No participant, quote, score, commercial outcome or buying signal should be entered unless it came from an actual validation interaction and has a source/date.

## Evidence rules

- Use one row per completed independent participant.
- Do not infer participant role, authority, budget, frequency or pain from job title alone.
- Record participant language verbatim only where permission allows; otherwise paraphrase and mark it as such.
- Preserve contradictory findings; do not average away disconfirming evidence.
- Separate observed facts from interpretation.
- Never convert interview sentiment into WTP, pricing, market size or product-market-fit evidence.
- Remove or redact personal information that is not necessary for the validation decision.
