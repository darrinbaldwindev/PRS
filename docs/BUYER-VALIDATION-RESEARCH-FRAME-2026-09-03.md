# PRS — Buyer Validation Research Frame

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Owner:** Marketing Overseer
**Status:** HYPOTHESIS / VALIDATION FRAME

## Target buyer hypotheses

1. Engineering/project leaders responsible for autonomous AI work.
2. AI platform/operations leaders managing multiple agents and providers.
3. Organisations that need evidence of what autonomous work actually did before accepting/releasing it.

## Pain hypotheses

- Evidence is fragmented across agents, repositories and CI.
- Project status can be asserted without sufficient verification evidence.
- Autonomous work creates a need for provenance, readiness and drift detection.
- A governance/control layer may prevent teams from scaling agent work confidently.

## Validation questions

- What autonomous work is currently difficult to trust?
- What evidence must exist before a team accepts/release work?
- Where does verification happen today?
- What failures or rework result from missing evidence?
- Who owns the problem and budget?
- Would an independent assurance layer be preferred over additional execution tooling?
- What minimum proof would justify adoption?

## Gate

These are hypotheses, not customer facts. Do not convert them into website claims until validated through interviews, observed workflows or equivalent evidence.
